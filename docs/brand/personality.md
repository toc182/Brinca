# Brand personality & voice

How Brinca speaks. Microcopy strings (CTA labels, toasts, celebrations, errors, form validation, empty-state copy) live in `microcopy.md`.

---

## Personality adjectives

- **Playful-with-purpose** — fun is the delivery mechanism, not the goal; every bounce is measured
- **Warm** — feels like a trusted coach or therapist, not a corporate dashboard
- **Clear** — every number, streak, and progress bar is legible to a parent, defensible to a clinician, understandable to a 9-year-old
- **Bilingual-by-birth** — Spanish and English are equal citizens; neither is a translation afterthought

---

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Audience | Tone | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kid-visible (results, celebrations) | Child 7–12 | High-energy, short words | Tú |
| Adult config & setup | Parent / coach | Calm, direct, informative | Tú |
| Clinical / therapist views | Therapist | Neutral, precise, zero emoji | Usted |
| Celebrations | Child (adults see) | Bright, specific, celebrate action not identity | Tú |
| Errors & gentle misses | Anyone | Kind, blameless, actionable | Tú |
| Upgrade nudges | Adult only | Benefit-led, soft, one CTA | Tú |

> **Register rule:** default `Tú` everywhere. Switch to `Usted` only on clinician, legal, billing, and verification surfaces. Never mix registers on a single screen.
